# Contribution terms

A separately signed Contributor License Agreement is not required for ordinary
issues or pull requests under the current license.

The contribution terms are published in [LICENSE](LICENSE), section 5, and
explained in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Contributors retain ownership
and grant the stated non-exclusive rights; there is no automatic copyright
assignment or prior-invitation requirement. Existing separately signed
agreements continue to govern the contributions they cover.
